# Roast My Resume

Paste resume text for a roast that makes you laugh and feedback that helps you improve.

## The Unique Kernel
The split is the product: a playful roast that makes people want to share the result, paired with constructive feedback that makes the experience genuinely useful. Feedback must quote the person's actual resume, explain the issue, and offer a rewrite; it must not invent accomplishments or facts.

## Who It's For
Students, freshers, and people applying for their first or second job—especially someone anxious about job hunting who wants both a laugh and useful resume feedback.

## The Core Loop
On one screen, paste resume text and click once. Get a short roast beside Real Talk suggestions that quote resume text and explain useful improvements, with the matching rewrites in a separate section below. Include all distinct, actionable improvements the resume supports rather than stopping at three.

## Inspiration & Identity
Playful but not cruel on the roast side; calm, clear, and constructive on the “Real Talk” side. The contrast between the two panels is central to the experience.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner cares about job hunting and the stress around resumes among their peers. They want a small, achievable project with a naturally shareable result.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Paste the sample resume described in the project conversation. The app returns a resume-specific roast and all distinct, actionable improvements supported by the text, prioritized by importance. Each Real Talk item quotes a real line and explains why it could improve; the matching rewrite appears in its own section below. If a rewrite needs facts absent from the resume, it uses an unmistakable placeholder instead of fabricating details. The result demonstrates the split between funny and genuinely useful; the top priority is clear. The page also includes a truthful note about how pasted text is handled.

## The POC Boundary
One page and one paste-to-results flow: resume text in, a roast, prioritized quote-and-explanation suggestions, and a separate section of matching rewrites out. Include all distinct, actionable suggestions the text supports without imposing a fixed count. Text only; no accounts, file uploads, or database. No invented facts in feedback. Keep the work small enough to build and demonstrate in a short session.

## Later
No later features identified yet.

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts and saved profiles: not needed to demonstrate the one-screen core loop.
- File uploads: paste-only keeps the first version small and achievable.
- Persistent database storage: unnecessary for this proof of concept.
