THE PROOF OF CONCEPT BOUNDARY

Now

One-page, text-only paste-to-results flow. Side-by-side roast and Real Talk, followed by matching rewrites. Include all useful, distinct improvements without a fixed count or fabricated resume facts.

Later

No later features identified yet. Keep this open for ideas that emerge after the core demo works.

Cut from v1
  • Accounts and saved profiles: not needed for the core loop.
  • File uploads: paste-only keeps the experiment manageable.
  • Persistent database: unnecessary for this proof of concept.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The learner cares about the stress of job hunting and resumes among their peers. The project should stay small enough to finish, while giving people a laugh and feedback they can actually use.

Deployment is optional. The goal is a working, demonstrable proof of concept—not a complete product.
